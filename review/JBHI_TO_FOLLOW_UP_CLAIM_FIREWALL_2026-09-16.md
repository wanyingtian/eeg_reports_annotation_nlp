# Claim firewall: current JBHI revision versus follow-up methods paper

**Purpose:** land the current eight-page combined JBHI revision without making
it depend on, absorb or preview the newly completed evidence-schema study.

## What remains in O8

The current revision can state that:

- the five-category, four-level, grammar-constrained classification framework
  was applied to a frozen MedGemma configured system;
- the report sets, reference annotations and classification endpoints were held
  fixed for the configured-system comparison;
- the MedGemma classification result was mixed and included the reported focal
  epileptiform sensitivity--precision trade-off;
- the full-cohort MedGemma portability analysis was classification-only; and
- neither explanation equivalence nor a model-weight or architecture effect is
  established.

These statements were already supported before the new factorial study and
have been accepted in Chris's author review. The new result strengthens the
reason for the boundary but does not require new prose or numbers in O8.

One durable sentence describes the division accurately if a later editorial
pass needs it:

> Evidence extraction is treated as a separately configured module and was not
> part of the contemporary-model portability endpoint.

The present O8 wording already conveys this meaning. Adding the sentence is
therefore optional, not a submission requirement.

## What stays out of O8

- The 20-report model-by-evidence-schema factorial.
- The 97/68% and 57/75% evidence-coverage values.
- The 47-point and 36-point descriptive interactions.
- Any suggestion that one model has better explanations or grounding.
- The new evidence-profile registry and compatibility checks.
- The blinded technical-reader result or future EEG-qualified review.
- Any full-cohort explanation-quality proposal.

These items answer a different methodological question and would enlarge the
revision after author review. They belong to the follow-up paper.

## What the follow-up may claim now

On a purposive 20-report development surface, evidence-schema changes moved
coverage and unchanged-quotation endpoints in opposite directions for the two
configured local models. The result demonstrates that the evidence interface
must be treated as a first-class experimental factor. It does not establish a
population effect, clinical explanation quality or model-family superiority.

## Communication rule for the current submission

Do not ask the JBHI author group to decide on this follow-up experiment while
they are reviewing O8. If it must be acknowledged, one sentence is sufficient:

> Separate development work on evidence-extraction interfaces is being retained
> for a follow-up methods study and does not change the attached classification
> revision.

Otherwise, leave it out of the submission communication entirely.
